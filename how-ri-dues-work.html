Rotary International Club Invoices are based on membership as of 1 January. This calculator produces a planning estimate that follows that snapshot for club treasurers — it is not the official invoice.

“Your Club Invoice is based on your membership count as of 1 January.” Keep My Rotary membership updated by 31 December. — Adapted from Rotary’s guidance; official Club Invoice FAQ

Key terms

Community-Based
Club base billed at $8 USD annual RI dues per member on this estimate.
University-Based
Club base billed at $5 USD annual RI dues per member on this estimate.
Invoice year
The January–December period you select; the invoice date is 1 January of that year.
Pro Rata Dues
Dues for months from the prior calendar year that were not billed on the previous January invoice: (Annual ÷ 12) × unbilled months, typically on the next January invoice for mid-year joiners.

Join timing → outcome on this invoice

How join timing affects estimated dues
Join timing Result on selected January invoice
Active on 1 January (joined before prior year, or Jan 1 of invoice year) Full-year Annual Dues
Joined mid-year in the prior calendar year Full-year Annual Dues + Pro Rata Dues for unbilled months in the prior year
Joined after 1 January of the invoice year $0 on this invoice; Pro Rata Dues start next January
Left before 1 January of the invoice year $0 or Pro Rata Dues only, depending on join year
Leave on or after 1 January of the invoice year No reduction on that January invoice (snapshot already taken)

Tax and local currency

The calculator applies your configured tax percentage separately to Annual Dues and Pro Rata Dues (USD) amounts, then converts using your USD-to-local rate for local totals. Adjust these in Invoice Summary on the calculator.
