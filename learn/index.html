    Stage 3 of 8

    How many months count?

    For a January 2027 invoice, Pro Rata Dues covers months in 2026 after the member joined. Count from the 1st of each month.

    If someone joins on the 1st, that month counts. If they join later in the month, start from the next month.

    1 July 2026 Joined on the 1st
    17 August 2026 Joined after the 1st
    3 October 2026 Joined after the 1st

    Stage 4 of 8

    Annual and per-month dues (USD)

    You chose your club base. Enter only those rates. Every money amount on this worksheet must be rounded to two decimals.

    Monthly dues are your annual USD divided by 12, then rounded to the nearest cent. Enter exactly two decimals, not the long division result. Later stages reuse this rounded figure, so a wrong cent here makes the final invoice wrong.

    USD rates

    Stage 5 of 8

    Convert to local currency

    Congratulations. You got the USD rates right. This worksheet uses a sample rate of $1 = 96 in local currency. Multiply your rounded USD amounts by 96.

    Use the two-decimal USD figures from the last stage. Annual local currency is annual USD times 96. Monthly local currency is the rounded monthly USD times 96. Keep two decimals again.

    Stage 7 of 8

    Individual dues in local currency

    Use the rounded annual and monthly local-currency amounts you already worked out. Active members get the annual amount for 2027. Pro Rata Dues are the monthly amount × that member’s unbilled 2026 months (months not billed on the previous January invoice). Keep two decimals.

    Each card already shows that member’s unbilled 2026 months. Those months were not on the Jan 2026 invoice, so they appear as Pro Rata Dues here. Annual is only if they are active on 1 January 2027. Pro Rata can still apply if they left later in 2026. Keep two decimals.

    Final table (Jan 2027 invoice)

    This is the same layout as the club invoice. Fill quantity, unit price, and total on each line. For this sample, local tax is 18%.

    Use your roster totals and the local-currency rates you already checked. Quantity times unit price equals the line total. For this sample, tax is 18% of each matching dues line, not 18% of the grand total. Previous balance is a carryover from earlier invoices: unpaid dues add to the bill; a credit or overpayment reduces it. This sample club has none, so enter 0.
