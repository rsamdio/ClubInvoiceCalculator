Understanding RI Dues

Learn about Rotary International dues structure and calculation principles

Ensure Accurate Invoicing for Your Rotaract Club

Your Club Invoice is based on your membership count as of 1 January.

To ensure accurate invoicing, please maintain an up-to-date member list on My Rotary.

Update Deadline: Please make sure to register new members and remove any terminated members by 31 December.

Why it Matters: Your invoice is calculated based on the number of active members in Rotary's database on 1 January.

No Adjustments: Please note that invoices cannot be adjusted once issued. Your club is responsible for paying the full amount shown on the invoice.

Important Notice: You should register new members and remove terminated members within 30 days of the effective date for that change. Please keep this in mind as you approach the 31 December deadline.

Log in to My Rotary to update your member list and ensure accurate billing.

Dues Structure & Calculation

Annual membership dues and how they are calculated for different scenarios

Annual Dues Rates

Community Based Clubs $8.00/member/year
University Based Clubs $5.00/member/year

How This Calculator Bills (Jan 1 Snapshot)

Members active on 1 January: Billed full-year annual dues for the invoice year (Jan–Dec).
Join after 1 January of the invoice year: This invoice shows $0 for that member. Catch-up (proration) appears on the next January invoice.
Prior-year mid-year joiners: Next January invoice includes full year plus prorated catch-up for months in the prior year. Formula: (Annual ÷ 12) × months.

Proration & Important Dates

How join and leave dates affect dues calculation and key principles

Join & Leave on This Invoice

Join on 1 January of the invoice year: Full-year dues on that invoice (no catch-up the following year for that join).
Leave after the invoice date: Does not reduce that January invoice. Leaving before 1 January can result in $0 or catch-up-only depending on join year.
Leave on 1 January: Still treated as active for that invoice (generated as of Jan 1).

Key Dates & Considerations

Join Date: Drives whether this invoice shows full year, catch-up, or $0.
Leave Date: Matters mainly when the member left before the Jan 1 invoice snapshot.
Invoice Year: The January invoice year you are estimating (upcoming Jan–Dec RI period).

How to Use the Calculator

This tool helps you estimate your club's Rotary International membership dues invoice from a member roster.

1 Set Invoice Summary

  • Invoice Year: Choose the year. Dues are calculated as of January 1.
  • Tax & Rate: Set your tax percentage and USD to local currency rate.
  • Live totals: The summary updates when you change settings or the roster.

2 Add Members

  • Individual: Enter name, club base (Community or University), join date, and leave date if needed.
  • Bulk Upload: Import an Excel or CSV file using the template columns.
  • Google Sheets: Copy the template, share it so anyone with the link can view, then paste the link to import.

3 Review Your Roster

  • Table: Search, page through, and check dues for each member.
  • Edit / Remove: Update or delete rows as needed.
  • Reset Roster: Clears everyone from this session after you confirm.

4 Save to Cloud (optional)

  • Sign In: Use Sign in at the top to connect your Google account.
  • Save to Cloud: Click Save to Cloud when you want to back up. Nothing is saved automatically.
  • Unsaved / Saved: The button shows Unsaved when you have changes, and Saved after a successful save.
  • On sign-in: If you already have cloud data, choose whether to keep this session or load from the cloud.

5 Download PDF

  • Download Planning Estimate (PDF): Downloads a planning PDF with summary totals and the full roster. Not RI's official invoice.
  • Estimate only: Use this for planning. Final figures come from Rotary's official invoice process.

Quick Tips

  • Double-check join dates before you generate a PDF. They drive proration.
  • If a member joins on 1 January of the selected invoice year, they are billed full-year dues on that invoice. If they join later in the year, this invoice shows $0 and catch-up starts next year.
  • Google Sheets import only works if the sheet is shared so anyone with the link can view it.
  • On another device, sign in and load your cloud data to continue a saved roster.
